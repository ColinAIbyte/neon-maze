Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

In one line: this game collects almost nothing about you. Apart from the nickname and score you choose to submit to the leaderboard, everything stays in your own browser.

1. What stays in your browser (never uploaded)

The following is kept in your browser's local storage. We cannot see it and it is never sent to any server:

  • Your local game records
  • The mute setting
  • Your language choice
  • Stars and unlock progress

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it permanently. It does not sync to another browser or another device.

2. What is uploaded, and only if you confirm it

Only after you enter a nickname on the result screen and confirm is that run submitted to the global leaderboard: nickname, score, level reached, highest combo, whether you cleared the game, and the submission time.

A randomly generated anonymous player ID is sent with it, used to attribute the score and for basic validation. It is not an account and does not sync across devices.

No confirmation, no upload. Practice runs and Daily Challenge runs are never uploaded.

Please note: nicknames and scores are public. Anyone visiting the leaderboard can see them, and they are kept long-term. We do not ask for, and do not recommend, your real name or any contact details. We collect no email address, phone number, date of birth, location or any identifying information.

3. What we do not do

  • No tracking cookies
  • No advertising, and no ad tracking code
  • We never sell or trade your data
  • No profiling, no cross-site tracking
  • No account required

The site contains an interface for analytics, but it is currently switched off and sends data to no analytics service. If that ever changes, we will update this page first.

4. Language detection

The site shows English by default. You can switch language at any time with the toggle, and your choice is remembered locally. That decision happens entirely inside your browser — nothing is sent out, and we never look up your IP location.

5. Third-party services involved

No other third party has access to your data.

6. About children

This is a casual game and we know children play it.

  • We do not actively collect any identifying information
  • Leaderboard nicknames are chosen by the player, and we recommend not using a real name
  • If you are a parent and your child submitted an inappropriate nickname, email us at the address below and we will remove it

7. Removing your records

To remove your entry from the leaderboard, email us with the nickname and roughly when it was submitted, and we will take care of it.

Locally stored data needs no request — clearing your browser's site data removes it.
